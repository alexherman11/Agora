The meeting QR code

You print one code per meeting from the Invites page you already have. It lets people talk straight away, and asks for an account only when they come back.

At the meeting

Scan, type a name

The phone opens the board with one box: "Your name". No email, no password. They can read and send in the open chats.

Their messages

Marked as guest

Their name shows with a small "guest" tag, so everyone knows it is not a checked account.

After

It runs out

The guest pass lasts until the end of the day after the meeting. Then the board is read-only for them, with "Sign in to keep talking".

When they return

Make an account, keep their words

If they sign up on the same phone, their guest messages move onto the new account. Otherwise the messages stay, tagged guest.

If someone misuses a code, you freeze it and every guest from it goes quiet at once, the way the invite links already work. Guests get a slower send limit than members, so one phone can't flood a chat.

Chats that feel like Discord

In the first version

  • Messages grouped by person, names and times, live as they arrive
  • Reply to a message (shows the quoted line; click jumps to it)
  • Emoji reactions, edit and delete your own, "(edited)"
  • @mentions, highlighted, and they notify the person
  • Unread: bold channels, a count, a red "New" line where you left off
  • Paste or drag images and files in (members)
  • Basic formatting: bold, italic, code, links
  • Pinned messages per channel; leaders remove messages
  • On a phone, the channel list slides in from the left

Later, if people want them

  • Threads off a message
  • "Someone is typing"
  • Direct messages between two people
  • Search inside a chat (the board's search already covers posts)
  • Slowmode per channel (the rule already exists underneath, it just needs a switch)

Making channels by describing them

Signed in as a leader, you get a box at the bottom of the channel list. You write what you want, like "a private chat for the legal team: me, Maya and Sam". Your agent turns that into the steps, shows them to you as one card with Make and Cancel, and runs them when you press Make. Every one can be undone. It works for more than channels: renaming, reordering, who can post, a new kind of post.

It uses your AI subscription. Members and guests never need AI for anything.

What changes from today's board

  • Today the board is a feed of posts with threads, like Reddit. Chats become Discord-style streams. Announcements and Calendar stay as they are, readable by anyone.
  • Today nobody outside your PC can reach the board. For the QR code to work at a meeting it has to be on the internet, at seabright.example.org.
  • Underneath, the kernel needs three general pieces, each usable by any community: a read by anyone setting for things (so visitors can see announcements), a guest person who lasts until a set time, and chat messages that can be edited, reacted to and removed. The chat word itself already exists.
