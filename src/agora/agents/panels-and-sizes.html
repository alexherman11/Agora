Three words, all general

    

      
sizes narrow 0, middle 1080, wide 1460
        
On the root of any lens. It names window widths. The window's current name is $view.size and its width is $view.width. Any part can use them, for example only when $view.size = wide. This is how each app says what it does at different dimensions.

      
panel left|right, icon, label
        
On any part. The part becomes a side panel that is open or shut, and shut means no width at all. Its icon goes in the window's top bar: left panels on the left and right panels on the right, like Claude desktop.

      
shut when narrow middle
        
On a panel. At those sizes it shuts itself, so the text stays in the centre. A panel you shut by hand stays shut when the window grows. A panel the size shut opens again when there is room.
