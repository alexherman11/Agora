# Writing (kernel-lab app)

*Builder draft by Claude, 27 Oct 2026, from Alex's ask: "lets make a new writing center, closely modeled off the old one but using the new component style", with two plans moved in "for me to review". Not Alex's words unless quoted.*

The old desk's Writing app (lenses `a5911 writing` and `a6079 doc-page`, exported in `old-desk/`) rebuilt as an ordinary component lens on the kernel desktop. It holds nothing of its own: documents and comments are world things, and every press says the line it runs.

## What Alex sees

- **On the dock: Writing** (a `button` with `does=window.open writing`). One click opens the window. It wears the place's theme like every window (Dusk today).
- **The rail**, as the old app had it: *Find a document* (title and text, as you type; `Ctrl+K`), then folding collections: **Plans** (Waiting, Answered; renamed from "Designs for me"), **Notes**, **Agora documentation**, **Personal writing**, **Files and unsorted** (uploaded files and documents in no collection; this replaces "Paragraph documents", which has no counterpart here). `+` on a collection starts a document in it. Newest first.
- **The top bar**: Back (`Alt+←`), Home, **New document**.
- **Home** (nothing open): plans waiting for you as cards, recently changed, a way to start.
- **A document** (4 Nov: one continuous editor, see below; before, each part was edited in its own box): its title (edit in place; Enter goes into the text), who made it and when, *Draft by Claude* where it is Claude's, its live word count, *Saved* / *Saving…* / *Not saved: why*, a link to its design page. The text is one page, as in Obsidian: click anywhere for the caret, drag to select across headings and lists, and the Markdown marks show only where the caret is. `Ctrl+E` shows every mark. `[[` offers documents by title and writes `[[id|Title]]` (the id never shows); `[[Title]]` and `[[FILE-NAME]]` resolve too, and a link to nothing yet starts that document. Task boxes tick in the text. **Linked from** lists every document that links here.
- **A plan** has Alex's call on it, as the old design review did: *Build it*, *Build it, with my comments*, *Not yet, I have questions*, *Wrong shape*, and *Back to waiting*. *Still open* shows what is not answered.
- **Comments**: select words, right-click: **Comment** puts a note beside them in the margin; it says *under § heading*, *moved*, *changed* or *gone* as the text changes (the old desk's ONE RULE, measured on the drawn words). Reply, Resolve, Reopen.
- **JEFF**: right-click selected words (or a part), **Send to JEFF**; or **Send to JEFF** on the whole document; or **JEFF** on a comment. It says a line in his **Conversation**, as Alex, with the passage quoted and a `[[link]]` back, and `about=` the document. **Sent to JEFF** under the document lists what went.
- Right-click menus end with the world's *Ask for a change…* box (`right_click=ask`), as every painter menu does.

## Things and words

| Thing | Facts |
|---|---|

| `kind=note` | the world's notes (the Rough spots) show under Notes and edit the same way |
| `kind=comment` (new, declared with help) | `about=` the document, `quote`, `before`, `after`, `heading`, `text`, `reply_to`, `resolved=yes` |

Why `document`: it already exists and means what this is; the files module also makes `document`s for uploaded files' pages, which the rail shows read-only under *Files and unsorted*. Why a new `comment`: no kind meant a remark on some words (`board.reply` is for posts; `say` drops `quote=`).

Gesture lines (all in `writing.lens`): `edit $d.id changes=… base=… version=…` (typing, 4 Nov), `set $d.id text=…` (kept for other writers), `set $d.id title=…`, `set $d.id collection=…`, `set $d.id plan=answered verdict=…`, `make kind=document …`, `make kind=comment about=$d.id quote=… text=…`, `say Conversation text=… about=$d.id`, `set $c.id resolved=yes`, `forget $d.id`. Typing is sent as changed spans with the text they were made against (`edit`); a change made meanwhile is merged under them (see below). Other gestures carry the document's revision, as before.

## One continuous editor (4 Nov)

*Builder draft by Claude, 4 Nov 2026, from Alex: "honestly lets just make it so its a continous document for now, i need easy editing, get rid of rectangles around text, make it a crdt, dont worry about same doc collaboration, just focus on making documents work more like a regular document editor. I should be able to highlight over sections, put my cursor anywhere etc. Think through normal doc editor stuff, and then make sure it works. I need it to work about as well as obsidian".*

- **The editor is CodeMirror 6**, what Obsidian's editor is built on, bundled into the `doc-page` component (the window's frame may load no outside script). A live preview draws the Markdown: headings large without `#`, bullets as dots, tasks as boxes, quotes with a bar, code in a band, tables as tables until the caret enters them, `[[links]]` by their title. Marks come back only for the element the caret is in (a heading's `#` for its line); a selection shows marks only at its moving end, so text does not jump while dragging. No boxes or card around the text.
- **Normal editing**: caret anywhere by click; drag, Shift+click, double and triple click, `Ctrl+A`; Backspace over a selection across sections; `Ctrl+Z`/`Ctrl+Y` (undo survives saves, repaints, and opening another document and coming back; up to 8 documents keep their editors); `Ctrl+B`, `Ctrl+I`, `Ctrl+Shift+X` and ``Ctrl+` `` mark the selection (again to unmark); Enter continues a list, Enter on an empty item ends it; Tab indents (a list item as an item) and Shift+Tab outdents, and the caret stays in the text; `Ctrl+F` find and replace; paste as text; pressing below the text puts the caret at the end; right-click on a selection: Comment, Send to JEFF, Copy, Cut (with nothing selected, the browser's own menu: spelling, paste); a drawn link opens on a press (with the caret in it, a press only places the caret).
- **It keeps itself**: 0.7 s after typing stops, and at once on `Ctrl+S`, leaving the editor, pressing a link or closing the window. What goes is the changed spans (`write`, the kernel's new `edit` word) with a fingerprint of the text they were made against. Typing during a save is kept and sent next.
- **The CRDT** (kernel `bodies.py`, a source edit): every document written with `edit` gets a Loro document beside the world (`<world>/bodies/<id>.text.loro`, appended per save, folded into one snapshot every 400), with who and when for each change; `edits <id>` reads it. The `text=` fact stays the truth everyone reads: an edit ends as an ordinary `set` (every check `set` makes, undo, `hear:set`). If someone else wrote the text meanwhile (JEFF, undo, another window), the edit merges into it through the body (`version=`) when it can, or is refused as stale and the page puts the other change under what you typed and sends again. The body follows any other writer before the next edit, so it never disagrees with the world. Deleted words live only in the body, which nothing serves.
- **Not done** (Alex: "dont worry about same doc collaboration"): no shared cursors and no live sync between two open windows of one document (the other window catches up at its next paint and merges); comments still anchor by quoted words, not CRDT cursors.

## Rough spots (frictions; source outside this folder: `kernel/bodies.py` and two lines in `kernel/words.py`, 4 Nov)

1. **A Conversation line cannot carry a quote.** `say` keeps `about=` but drops `quote=`, so the passage travels inside the text as a Markdown quote. Missing: `say … quote=` (or `about=` plus a quoted span) that jeff-chat can draw as "Answering: …". The conversation-draft's `quote`/`reply_to` is only for answering a said line, so the window sends rather than preparing JEFF's composer.
2. **No word opens a web address.** A plan's design page (claude.ai) can only be shown and copied. Missing: `window.open url=<https…>` for the person's own browser, admitted per person.
3. **The dock draws a plain plate for Writing.** The dock picks an icon from the lens name (`shell-kit.js` `appOf`); a button's `icon=` fact is not read. Missing: an `icon=` the dock honours, or an app-icon thing.
4. **`api.part` writes `null` attributes as the string "null"** (`readonly="null"` made the title read-only). The components avoid passing nulls. Missing: the painter's `el` skipping null and false, as most element helpers do.
5. **A said line is not bound into the view**, so sending to JEFF repaints nothing; the page re-reads `rows said about=` itself, uncached (`api.rows`), because `api.read` is cached per paint.
6. **Editing focus survives a repaint, `change` does not.** The painter hands a focused input's value to the new element, which then never fires `change`; the title saves on Enter and blur instead.
7. **No exclusion in a lens query.** The old "Notes" was `-prop:collection`; here `prop:collection=` (empty) matches the absent fact, which is enough, but there is no `-prop:` for "not this value".
8. **Plans' state is a fact the page sets, not a workflow.** "Answered except T1" is `plan=answered still_open=…`; a per-question state would need each question as its own thing.
9. **No word changed a text by its changed spans** (4 Nov, source edit). Saving a typed change meant resending the whole text (`set text=`), which cannot merge with another writer and records the whole text each time. Added: the kernel's `edit <thing> changes= base= [field=] [version=]` and `edits <thing>`, general for any text fact, with a CRDT history beside the world (`kernel/bodies.py`). Missing still: a world-level way to say a kind's text field is prose (the "value kinds say how they combine" idea in ONE-DOCUMENT-KIND.md), so `set` itself could merge.
10. **A component cannot own long-lived work** (4 Nov). The painter (`isolation.js`) scopes every timer, frame callback and listener made while a part paints to that paint, and drops them at the next one; a library such as CodeMirror schedules its own work and cannot know. Worked around by making the editor from a promise (`wrLater`, `wrAfter`). Missing: an `api.persist(fn)` (or a documented scope outside the paint) for state that outlives repaints, and a way to keep one element (an editor) across repaints instead of moving it into each new page.
11. **A script's click is not a press** in the headless window: `button.click()` on a rail row did not open the document; real input events do. `editor_proof.py` presses with real mouse events throughout.
