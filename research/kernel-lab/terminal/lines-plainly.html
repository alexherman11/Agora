  • Short first screen. Who you are, how a line works, the 15 words most worth knowing, and the topics. help all is the old full list, each word once.
  • help <anything>. A word, a topic (windows, theme, tasks, music, who…), a module, a kind, one thing by id; else the words that mention it. It never ends at "nothing answers".
  • Topics are world things. Twelve pages made with make kind=topic; anyone fixes one with set <id> text=…. Every help page ends with the line that fixes it.
  • help check lists help that misleads (20 words today, for example task-add's example said task.add).
  • Word names work in word-set, which now echoes what changed. Aliases show on a word's page.
  • Refusals say what to type: window.move Lines to=left → "Lines is dk74 (a window). Say: window.move dk74 to=left". Typos get "did you mean".
  • A bug: task-add without who= stored who=$who on the card. Settings nobody gives are now left out (your board had one: fixed).
