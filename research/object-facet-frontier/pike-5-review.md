## 3. My position on 8.1

**Printed arithmetic, one operator per `derive`, no nesting.**

- An expression becomes named steps: `derive per = $b.pages / $club.pace`, then `derive wk = ceil $per`.
- Each step prints inputs and result, and each is fenced on what it read. That removes 8.1's danger ("a second syntax nobody can read once nested"): nothing nests.
- `running` is the one aggregate.
- Dates take units (`+ 7d`).
- Named words stay for meaning (`weekday`, `every`), and `where`/`pick` are structural.
- No evaluator until an everyday task needs a conditional inside a derivation, or more than five chained steps for one value.
