## Missing boundary / proposed next decision

The native send only checks selected capture IDs; it does not check pending.
World `only when` or `waits` is a scene snapshot, not atomic admission.
An asynchronously published pending token can arrive after Send. The draft note
is reused across messages, so its ID alone is not a message-generation binding.
`_change` uses separate examine/prop requests; fake retries do not certify
concurrent process isolation.

The retained painter obtains a newer *entire action and fence* when sending.
That can substitute reply/audience/topic provenance while words remain held;
focus clears `_next` and can lose its freshest snapshot. Its six-second wait
also awaits an unbounded refresh; rejected refresh lacks wait cleanup.
These are source findings, not browser reproductions.

Recommend reviewing a narrow extension of existing context_from admission:
compare a draft/message-generation and reservation identity, refuse unresolved
reservations before append, consume exactly the admitted captures in the existing
native mutation boundary. Keep the original audience/reply/topic provenance;
do not substitute arbitrary fresh action/fence. Define release/Send ordering
and late registration explicitly with the existing capture host/house path;
do not claim that polling alone guarantees immediate drag-end binding.
The bounded painter wait needs its own timeout/error cleanup.

This extends beyond the card's stated host-marker-only source direction.
Sable/JEFF should decide that narrow source boundary before more implementation.
No extra service, authority, host route or ordinary chat refusal change proposed.
Then use an isolated fake feed and admitted job-owned Guard consumer, at both
1280 and 2560, for pending/joined-send/draft/context/caret acceptance.
