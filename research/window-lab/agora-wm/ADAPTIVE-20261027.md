## Read: causes and scope

`engine.solve` previously only reduced the incoming window before evicting existing windows. Existing tiles were never repacked on open. `manager.ingest` also interpreted the native geometry transition from minimised to normal as a drag immediately after solving a restore; that could undo its safe placement and displace other windows. The new mocked regression exercises that transition. These are source findings, not proof that they explain every historical live disappearance. The live policy read was `live`, `minimise-oldest`.

## Implemented

- Keep already free placements; otherwise perform deterministic bounded repacking of visible managed windows before eviction, retaining pins/obstacles and learned minimum sizes. Try larger tiles first. If no arrangement is found, evict LRU unpinned windows, preserving the incoming one when feasible. Existing pin narrowing remains a last resort. No app quits.
- Store per-victim causal displacement stacks. Closing/minimising the opener releases only its debt. Restore exact geometry together when legal; otherwise restore individual legal placements, or reveal a minimised victim in available smaller space while retaining its debt. Blocked restoration waits for later closes/minimisations. Nested and out-of-order closes do not blindly roll back the screen.
- Explicit manual moves/resizes, minimise/restore and pin changes invalidate that victim's debt. Closed victims are removed; current bounds/minima/obstacles govern restoration. Startup/gate resume forgets stale debt and starts from actual geometry. Memory is deliberately session-local.
- Native restore geometry no longer enters the self-resizer/drag path.
