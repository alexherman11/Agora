# Trial 6: change the multiplicities and lifecycles

The working Trial05 modules are copied verbatim into trials/06-live-sandbox.
Same three authors own the same three files. No new shared application scaffold
or prearranged new IDs. Negotiate new public contracts directly with one another.
Only the generic interpreter is owned by the coordinator. First evolved programs
are frozen before tests, and each author repairs only their own declarations.

Motion: turn the fixed body population into an editable population. Create,
duplicate, select and delete bodies during use; offer a way to change the motion
of selected bodies while the clock runs. Keep pose/displacement composition and
stable glyph ports. A deleted seed must remain deleted across save/reload. Coordinate
selection cleanup and scenario restore with Instrument. New bodies from another
author's commands must automatically get your views and motion behavior.

Field: generalize one fixed attractor into multiple editable force sources. Their
forces compose over the same bodies. Create/delete/tune/drag individual fields;
derive connections and force diagnostics from live population. Do not keep hidden
single-main assumptions in force calculation, views, or invariants. New fields
introduced by scenario restore must work automatically. Coordinate current-body
selection controls and scenario protocol directly with Motion and Instrument.

Instrument: provide named saved scenarios and restore of a prior body/field
configuration, plus meaningful status explaining the loaded scenario. Coordinate
owned-state details with peers. Restoring must handle bodies/fields created or
deleted since capture, and future dynamic populations must not require hardcoded
record IDs. Keep transport and scene arrangement; add an alternate high-contrast
presentation suitable for inspection. In Trial05 Observatory, pale labels and
metrics were hard to read: fix the composition at the authored style layer.

All three: retain working peer ports unless directly agreed otherwise. Add cases
for your new invariants and adverse inputs. Do not edit peers or runtime. Prefer
existing joins and derivations. If source feels forced, state the exact missing
operation and a small counterexample; do not hide complexity in feature handlers.

Acceptance goes beyond individual authored cases: create/edit/delete objects
while running; independent forces really sum; delete-then-restore preserves the
captured configuration; no seed resurrection; scene choice and all controls remain
usable; active-author withdrawal has explicit dormant dependencies; simultaneous
disjoint edits proceed and incompatible edits retain conflicts. Browser behavior,
load boundaries, and failure histories are recorded separately from static checks.
