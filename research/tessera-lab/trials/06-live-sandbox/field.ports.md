# Field ports for the live sandbox

`fields` is an open collection. Every source has `label` (string), `x`/`y` (logical world coordinates) and signed `strength` (positive attractor, negative repulsor, zero off). The retained seed `fields/main` is ordinary and may be deleted. Commands are `field.create {id,label,x,y,strength}`, `field.delete {id}`, `field.place {id,x,y}`, `field.setStrength {id,value}` and `field.rename {id,label}`. `field.sources` lists the live population. Each source produces a `field.handle` occurrence keyed by source ID and a separate set of measured lines to nearby `motion.body` occurrences. The force reaction sums all live source contributions for every live body and adds its result to Motion's velocity counters.

Motion's `motion.poseX {id}` / `motion.poseY {id}` remain the physical position ports. Motion confirmed `selection/main.bodyId` and `motion.selectedBody {}`; Field does not pin a body ID for force or diagnostics. The current population is always queried. Field contributes to `motion.body.face` and attaches its tuners under Instrument's `instrument.controls`.

For scenarios, Instrument owns `scenario.capture {id,name}`, `scenario.restore {id}`, and `scenarios.name` / `scenarios.bodies`. Field declares `scenarios.fieldsSnapshot` and reacts to capture by co-creating that disjoint field on the same `scenarios/id`. Field reacts to restore by deleting unsaved sources, recreating missing saved sources and restoring fields on surviving sources. All effects join Instrument's body restore atomically. Instrument confirmed this protocol directly. No seed ID is special to restore.
